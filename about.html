    More Than Just a Tea Shop
    

At **ITU UNGAL KADAI**, we believe that tea is not just a drink — it is a part of everyday life.

Our goal is simple: serve delicious tea, tasty snacks, and create a welcoming place where everyone feels comfortable.

We carefully prepare every cup using quality ingredients and serve it hot and fresh. From traditional tea lovers to people looking for a quick evening snack, we have something for everyone.

Our shop is a place where friends meet, families spend time together, colleagues take a break, and everyone can enjoy a simple cup of tea with a smile.
